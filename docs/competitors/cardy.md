# Competitor review: Cardy (compared with Easy Quizy)

Written 2026-10-19. Sources: Cardy's App Store listing (Austria, via Apple's public lookup data), its 7 App Store
screenshots, and two screen recordings the author made of the app on her iPhone (the onboarding, the paywall, and
about five minutes of using the app, with the interface in Russian and German as the language being learned).
Easy Quizy's side is taken from `SPEC.md` and `HANDOFF.md`, not from reading the code again.

**What was not seen:** audio (nobody listened to it), animations and the feel of gestures (a recording cut into
frames cannot show them), whether Cardy's map lights up as you learn (the test account had 0 learned words), and
Cardy's website (its page returned an error). Nothing here comes from independent reviews: there are none.

## 1. Cardy at a glance

| | |
|---|---|
| App | "Cardy: Englisch lernen & mehr", subtitle "Deutsch, Griechisch & Vokabeln" |
| Who | One developer, based in Cyprus. First release July 2025, about 25 updates since (version 1.3.15 on 2026-10-16) |
| What | Flashcards for English, German and Greek, ready-made A1-C1 decks, FSRS scheduling |
| Languages | 12 interface languages. iPhone, iPad, Mac (Apple silicon) and Vision Pro. 70 MB, 4+ |
| Traction | **4.3 stars from 9 ratings** after 15 months |
| Price | Free with a subscription: 7 days free, then EUR 6.99 a month or EUR 49.99 a year ("40% saving"). The listing shows several more price points (up to 9.99 / 64.99), probably tests or other regions |
| Claims | "five minutes a day", no ads, no account needed to start, offline, cloud sync, driving mode, zen mode, widget (since 1.3.11) |

**The store page.** The title and subtitle carry the keywords. There are seven slides, each with a benefit headline
("Wörter lernen. Mehr behalten.", "Eigene Karten erstellen", "Dein Fortschritt im Blick", "Grammatik klar erklärt",
"Freihändig lernen im Auto-Modus", "Wiederholen mit weniger Ablenkung", "Angenehmer Dunkelmodus") above one
phone showing the screen. The look is light, generic and tidy. The store slides show a Greek deck, which gives the
impression of a Greek or English app; the German deck is only visible inside the app.

## 2. What happens inside Cardy

**Onboarding (about 10 steps, with a progress bar).**
1. Five swipeable intro cards: reminders at the right moment; words by topic and level (chips such as "Top 100",
   "Travel A1", "Food A2"); key facts on one card (synonyms, antonyms, examples, related words, origin, verb forms);
   a review plan with a bar chart; creating your own cards.
2. What you are learning, then your translation language (11 choices).
3. Why you need German (work or study, reading and films, travel, talking with people, other).
4. What bothers you most (I forget words fast, I don't know what to learn, I'm not regular, little time, I want to
   learn my own words).
5. A demo card (entdecken / discover) with a timeline: today, in 2 days, in a week.
6. Your level, A1 to B2, each with a one-line description.
7. What to learn: the top 1000 words, pick topics, or make your own cards.
8. Review mode: two buttons (don't remember / remember) or four (again, hard, good, easy), "can be changed later".
9. Time per day: 10, 20 or 30 minutes (35, 70 or 105 new words a week).
10. Reminder time (a slider from 06:00 to 21:00), then the system permission for notifications.
11. A "setting up" screen with a three-item checklist (considering your level and topics, picking starting words,
    forming the first queue), then "first cards ready".
12. **A paywall before any use:** unlimited reviews, unlimited card creation with AI pictures and pronunciation,
    offline access, no ads; 7 days free with a three-line timeline (today, day 5 reminder, day 7 trial ends).
    In the recording a red "could not start the subscription, try again" bar appeared at the bottom.

**Home.** "You have been learning words: 0 days", a Mon-Sun row of streak circles, a search field with a plus, four
small buttons (My words, Cheat sheets, statistics, settings) floating in empty space and a large "start learning"
button. It looks unfinished.

**Map.** A round "galaxy" of every word as blue dots, with rings for A1 (middle) out to C1 (edge). Pinch in until word
labels appear; tap a word for a small card (word, level, translation, example); tap the card for the full page
(related words, word formation, "add to queue"). There is a search bar and a recentre button. It browses the whole
language by level; it did not show progress in the recording.

**Study card.**
- Top bar: a daily goal ("0/100"), three counters (new, learning, review; tapping them explains the daily limits), and
  a menu with "report an error", "history" and "pause this word".
- Front: a line "A1 · verb · Top-1000 words", the German word with a speaker button (reflexive verbs show "(sich)"),
  the hint "try to recall what the word means, then flip the card" and a "show card" button.
- Back: the translation, **the verb forms** ("trifft · traf · hat getroffen"), an example with its English line, "add
  image", then four rating buttons with the next interval above each (1 m, 5 m, 15 m, 16 d).
- Some cards have a picture. The match is uneven: a toy car for "die Sache", a club for "der Verein".
- Only recognition (German to English). No typing, no gesture.

**Word sets.** Topics (economy, environment, health, emergencies, expressions, idioms, rooms, furniture, family, house,
food...) each split into A1 to C1 with a word count; grammar sets (verbs, nouns, adjectives, adverbs, prepositions
with the case they take); a **Goethe-Institut** set. Words show as "new" until studied; a checkbox adds a set.

**Cheat sheets.** Articles and cases (tables), pronouns, nouns, adjectives, verbs, tenses, word order, prepositions,
conjunctions, irregular verbs.

**Add a card.** Front, back, pronunciation (basic or AI), an image, a personal note with writing prompts ("any similar
words that confuse you? make a short sentence; how does the word connect to your life?"), a "learn both sides" switch.

**Statistics.** Total progress (0 / 13,320 words), progress per level (A1 2,724, A2 1,760, B1 3,788, B2 3,121; C1
is about 1,900 by subtraction), an activity heatmap, review history (today, 7 days, 30 days, 3 months), reviews per
hour, a forecast and a retention table (cards recalled and forgotten).

**Settings.** Language (learning German, knowing English), daily plan (10 new, goal 100), cards and voice-over (zen
mode, advanced), word sets, interface (system theme), widget and car, account (save progress), help.

**Free limits and pressure.** Free users get 10 new words and 50 reviews a day. At the end of a limited session a
"Premium -70%" offer appears with a live countdown ("ends in 23 h 57 min").

## 3. Where Easy Quizy stands

**We have, they do not:** the circle gesture; calibration from 15 words instead of a questionnaire and a level
picker; a word in front of you within a minute; exploring by association; a map that shows progress; a rating that
seeds the schedule; reflexive verbs with "sich" and a reflexive example; a distinctive look (night sky, one orange
accent); no paywall.

**They have, we do not:**

| Feature | Notes |
|---|---|
| **Verb forms on the card** (trifft, traf, hat getroffen) | Best find for a B1-B2 learner. The data is in Wiktionary, so it is a data job, not a design one. |
| **Level labels A1-C1** on cards and topics, and exam-style sets (Goethe-Institut) | We hide levels on purpose (tiers 1-6). A small label might fit; it is a principle to decide. |
| **Audio** on every card | In our spec, not built. |
| **Content size** | 13,320 German words against our 1,474. |
| **Pictures** on cards and your own cards | Large effort; the matches in Cardy are uneven. |
| **Grammar cheat sheets** | Articles and cases, tenses, word order, prepositions, irregular verbs. |
| **Streak row and daily goal** | A weekly row of circles, "0/100". |
| **Statistics over time** | History, forecast and retention. We deliberately dropped a dashboard; the Map is our answer. |
| **Pause a word, report an error** | We can only delete a word. |
| **Personal note with prompts** on your own cards | The spec has a "memory hook" field, not built. |
| **Intervals on the rating buttons** | Not a gap: our scheduling is hidden on purpose. |
| **Reminder asked during onboarding** | Ours is offered after the first study session. |
| **Widget, car mode, iPad and Mac, sync, light mode, 12 languages** | Out of scope for v1 (widget postponed). |
| **A store page that sells** | Keyword title and subtitle, seven benefit slides. Ours does not exist yet, and "Easy Quizy" never says "German". |

**What the map comparison says.** Both apps have a map, which supports the idea. Theirs is the whole language laid
out by level (radial), which you browse; ours is topics as stars that light up as you learn. Worth borrowing from
theirs: word labels once you zoom in, search on the map, a recentre button, and level rings as an optional layer.
The total number of words stays hidden on ours on purpose.

## 4. What I would do, in order

1. Verb forms on the card (data work first).
2. CEFR-style labels, decided together with the "no levels" principle.
3. Audio (check the source: on-device speech is free; recordings are better and larger).
4. The App Store page: German keywords in the title and subtitle, five to seven benefit slides.
5. A streak or weekly row and a daily goal, in the same quiet style as the rest.
6. A note field on your own words, and "pause word" and "report an error" in a small menu.
7. Map ideas: labels when zoomed in, search, a recentre button.
8. A short grammar reference (cases and articles first).
9. Pictures: skip for now.

## 5. A note on their weak points

A paywall before any use, tight free limits, a countdown discount, a bare home screen, a card that only tests
recognition, and a questionnaire of ten steps before the first word. Cardy has had 15 months, about 25 releases, a
store page and a subscription, and has 9 ratings: a competent copy of the usual flashcard approach does not get
found by itself, which suggests the idea matters more than matching their feature list. This last point is my
reading, not a fact from the data.
